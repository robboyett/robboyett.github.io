CRIMEWAVE

A location, rendered as techno. Live UK street-crime data drives a generative engine. Walk between places and the track morphs — one uninterrupted set.

Simulate a walk — tap a location to drift toward it
Enter a UK postcode or lat/long, or use your location, then read the area.
Source: data.police.uk open API — street-level crime, monthly, anonymised to ~1 mile. Data lags ~2 months, so this is the mood of a place, not a live feed.
Audio: Tone.js, generated entirely in-browser. Scotland reports only via British Transport Police, so crime there reads artificially low.
