PennyWise

A tactile digital tabletop for children to learn British currency through play.

PennyWise: The Tactile Money Table is a prototype money management game for kids. Instead of abstract numbers on a screen, children move, stack, and exchange coins and notes on a digital tabletop — building intuition for British currency through physical interaction.

Built with React and Vite. The experiment explores whether tangible manipulation on a flat surface can make financial literacy feel less like homework and more like play.
