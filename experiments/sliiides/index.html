Sliiides

Story-first presentation kit — write in Markdown, layer in custom HTML slides when they earn their place.

Most slide tools start with a blank canvas and ask you to fill it. Sliiides flips that: the narrative lives in talk.md, presenter notes are first-class, and custom HTML slides in slides/ are optional upgrades — not the starting point.

Every Markdown entry becomes a presentable slide immediately. Where visuals matter, any slide can become a standalone HTML document with its own CSS, JavaScript, canvas, or WebGL. Audience and presenter views stay synced across two browser windows. Zero build step — open index.html locally and go.

The structure is deliberately agent-ready: one canonical story file, explicit sequencing, and resilient placeholders while richer slides are still being built.
